History

TechCon began as a single-day meetup in 2012 and has since grown into a major annual conference focused on practical software engineering, ethical AI, and developer empowerment. Each year the event expands both its programming and community outreach efforts. Below are photos from landmark moments in TechCon history.

Mission

TechCon's mission is to accelerate learning and ethical innovation in technology by connecting practitioners, researchers, and students. We focus on actionable talks, reproducible workshops, and mentorship so attendees leave with concrete skills and new collaborators.

Past Speakers

Over the years TechCon has hosted leaders from startups, academia, and large tech firms. Below are a few notable speakers and short bios.

Lee Chen

Senior software architect known for scalable systems and open-source tooling. Lee presented a widely-cited talk on microservice observability and maintains several popular developer libraries.

Maria García

Researcher in human-centered AI. Maria's work focuses on fairness and interpretability in machine learning; she led a hands-on session at TechCon on designing inclusive datasets.

Sam Owens

CTO at a cloud startup. Sam spoke about engineering culture and ownership and ran a popular panel on sustainable engineering practices for high-growth teams.
